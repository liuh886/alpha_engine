# v4.2 SGOV recovery precursor — 50% TQQQ one-shot experiment

**Pre-registration date:** 2026-08-02  
**Status:** research-only; not trade-ready

## Question

The frozen 25% TQQQ precursor improved the blended QQQI/SGOV profile, but it was active for only six sessions across three events. This experiment asks one narrower question:

> On the exact same precursor dates, does increasing pre-state-2 TQQQ from 25% to 50% produce a better risk-adjusted result?

## Single admitted allocation

Ordinary blended state 1 remains:

- 25% QQQI;
- 25% SGOV;
- 50% QQQ.

When the frozen precursor is observable at the close, the next-open shadow allocation becomes:

- 0% QQQI;
- 0% SGOV;
- 50% QQQ;
- 50% TQQQ.

The extra 25% TQQQ is funded by removing the remaining 25% QQQI. Formal state 2 remains unchanged at 25% QQQ / 75% TQQQ.

No other weight is admitted. This is not a grid search.

## Frozen precursor

The precursor is inherited exactly from the prior experiment:

- shock memory active;
- QQQ medium repair complete;
- VIX normalized;
- VXN not stressed;
- secondary price confirmation not yet required.

The 50% and 25% variants must have exactly the same precursor dates.

## Co-primary evaluation

The 50% variant is judged against the 25% precursor on:

- full-sample CAGR and Sharpe;
- early- and late-sample CAGR;
- maximum drawdown;
- expected shortfall at 95%;
- worst 5-day and 20-day return;
- marginal result for each precursor event;
- concentration of marginal benefit;
- preservation of the five major v4.2 trough protections;
- recovery status of major episodes.

A higher CAGR alone is insufficient.

## Shadow gate

Non-actionable shadow monitoring can be authorized only when all conditions pass:

- at least six precursor events exist;
- at least 67% of events benefit from the extra 25% TQQQ;
- no event contributes more than 65% of positive marginal benefit;
- full, early and late CAGR are no worse than the 25% precursor;
- Sharpe is no more than 0.02 below the 25% precursor;
- maximum drawdown worsens by no more than 1.5 percentage points;
- expected shortfall worsens by no more than 0.75 percentage points;
- worst 5-day and 20-day results worsen by no more than 1.5 and 2.0 percentage points;
- at least four of five major v4.2 troughs remain improved;
- median trough protection remains at least 2 percentage points;
- no major episode remains unresolved.

Passing never changes Telegram or promotes the strategy directly.

## Stop rule

After this comparison, no further precursor weight is tested on the current sample. The result must choose among:

1. retain the 25% precursor as the deferred shadow hypothesis;
2. replace it with the 50% precursor as the deferred shadow hypothesis;
3. reject the precursor family if the marginal risk is disproportionate.
